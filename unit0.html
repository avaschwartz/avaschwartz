Four problems, four prototypes

Each prototype started with a problem someone had. Pick one to see the photos and how it helps.

Headphone stand Pencil holder Mini basketball hoop Shoe cover

Headphone stand

This headphone stand is used to keep headphones off a desk to prevent clutter. It also provides a designated place for easier access when charging your headphones.

Pencil holder

Someone mentioned that they had no place to store all their writing utensils at their desk. This pencil holder will help them organize their pens and pencils all in one place.

Mini basketball hoop

This mini basketball hoop is created for taking a break from homework and having some fun right at your desk. You just toss a small ball or crumpled piece of paper through the hoop to see how many shots you can make.

Shoe cover

A problem was raised about someone buying expensive shoes that they did not want to get dirty. This shoe cover will help prevent their shoes from becoming dirty.
